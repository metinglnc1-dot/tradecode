Same risk. Different size. Why?

Account $5,000. Risk 1% = $50.
Entry 100, stop 98: $2 per unit, so 25 units ($2,500 position).
Entry 100, stop 95: $5 per unit, so 10 units ($1,000 position).
If either stop is hit, you lose $50.

Size = risk ÷ stop distance. Wider stop, smaller position.
