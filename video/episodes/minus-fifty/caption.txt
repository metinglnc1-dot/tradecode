Lose 50%, then gain 50%, and you are still down 25%.

$1,000 drops to $500. A 50% gain brings it back to only $750. Percentages multiply, they don't add.

The gain you need to get back to even is 1 / (1 - loss) - 1:
-10% needs +11.1%
-25% needs +33.3%
-50% needs +100%

Down is the elevator. Up is the stairs.
