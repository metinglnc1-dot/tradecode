Same $1,000. Same trades. One of them pays 2.5x more in fees.

Market orders pay the taker fee. Limit orders that rest in the book pay the maker fee. On a typical regular tier that is 0.05% vs 0.02% per side.

At 10x on $1,000 of margin, the position is $10,000. One round trip costs $10 as a taker and $4 as a maker. Twenty trades a day: $200 vs $80.

The catch: a limit order can miss the move. Plan the entry instead of chasing it.
