I gave Claude my trading journal. It handed back one red card.

Your journal needs 6 columns: date, time, setup, risk, result, and how you felt.

Then paste your last 50 trades with this prompt:
"Here are my last 50 trades with date, time, setup, risk and result. Find the 3 patterns that cost me the most money. Be blunt. Then suggest one rule to test for the next 2 weeks."

The loop: keep the journal, ask for the patterns, change one rule, review in 2 weeks.
