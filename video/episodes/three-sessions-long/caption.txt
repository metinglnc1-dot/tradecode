The market has 3 personalities. Which one do you trade?

Asia is usually calm and often builds a tight range.
London is fast. Its open often takes one side of that range.
New York is the crowd. Its open, overlapping London, is the busiest part of the day.

Common ICT killzones (New York time):
Asia 20:00 to 00:00
London 02:00 to 05:00
New York 07:00 to 10:00

Check your own settings, daylight saving can shift them. Do not memorise the hours. Draw them on your chart. The ICT Killzones Sessions indicator draws them for you, link in bio.
