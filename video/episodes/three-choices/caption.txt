Same $500 win. Three traders. Which one are you?

A takes half the profit out and keeps the same risk.
B changes nothing.
C feels hot and risks 5x more on the next trade.

Then the next trade loses:
A: $10,250 - 1% = $10,147.50, plus $250 set aside
B: $10,500 - 1% = $10,395
C: $10,500 - 5% = $9,975, below the $10,000 start

Same win. Different choice. Comment A, B or C 👇
