This red candle looks like a crash. On the 1 minute chart, it is.

Zoom out to 15 minutes: a small dip. Zoom out to 4 hours: barely a wiggle in an uptrend.

Zoomed in, every wiggle feels like danger. Use the big timeframe for direction and the small one only for entries. Pick one main timeframe and stick to it, even when it's boring.
