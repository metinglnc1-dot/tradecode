Everyone is posting about the free Day Trader skill pack for Claude. Here is what it actually does, and what it cannot do.

It is an open source project on GitHub (search: day-trader-skill), MIT license, made by an independent developer. Not by Anthropic. It runs on free public data, no API key needed.

11 skills: chart scanner, news monitor, entry timer, exit planner, risk manager, budget manager, a time machine that replays a setup 10,000 times, price alerts, earnings reader, Congress trade tracker and an order sender that only sends the orders you approve.

What it cannot do: predict the price or promise a profit. It cannot place an order on its own. Paper trading is the default.
