Why does price hit your stop, then go your way?

Equal lows collect stops. That pile of stops is liquidity.
Price can dip, take it, and turn. That is a sweep.
But not every sweep turns. Wait for confirmation.

Put the stop a bit beyond the structure and make the position smaller for the same risk. Do not put your stop where everyone else does.
