If I had to restart with $1,000, this is the first rule: protect the account.

Risk 1% per trade. That is $10.
10 losses in a row at 1% of what is left: 0.99¹⁰ = 0.904. You still have about $904.

The rest of the list:
2 losses in a day? Close the platform.
One setup.
Every trade goes in the journal.
No trade outside the plan.

Not a profit target. A rule list. Save this for the day you start over 🔖

Numbers are illustrative.
