You sell your winners too early and hold your losers too long. It has a name: the disposition effect.

A study of 10,000 accounts at a large discount brokerage found a strong preference for selling winners rather than losers.

Your buy price becomes an anchor. Green feels like a gift to grab. Red feels like a loan that must come back. So small wins get locked in and losses grow.

Fix it at the entry: write your exit when you buy, not when it hurts.
