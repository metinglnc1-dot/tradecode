5 candles. Which one didn't you know?

1. Hammer: long lower wick. Buyers pushed back from the low.
2. Shooting star: long upper wick. Sellers pushed back from the high.
3. Engulfing: one body swallows the last one.
4. Doji: open and close almost equal. Indecision.
5. Inside bar: the whole candle fits inside the last one.

One candle without context is not a signal.

Drop the number you didn't know 👇
