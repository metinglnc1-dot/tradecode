Of the Brazilian day traders who kept going for more than 300 days, 97% lost money.

The study followed everyone who started day trading Brazilian equity futures between 2013 and 2015. Only about 1 in 100 (1.1%) earned more than the minimum wage. Staying longer did not save them. Persistence is not an edge.

In Europe, regulators report that 74 to 89% of retail CFD accounts lose money.
