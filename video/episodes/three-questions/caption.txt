Stop. Size. Plan. By the time you check all three, the move is gone.

The fix is not skipping them. Answer them before the chart opens:
1. Where is my stop?
2. If it hits, how many dollars do I lose?
3. Is this setup in my plan? If not, no trade.

Then the entry is one click, not a panic. The thinking is already done.
