Every trade has a tiny passenger: the fee goblin.

About 0.05% when you open, and again when you close. That is 0.1% of your position per round trip. At 10x leverage, that is 1% of your margin. Twenty trades a day? 20% of your margin, every single day.

Fewer trades. Limit orders when you can. Fees are the one loss you control.

Save this before your next scalp 🔖

Illustrative: $1,000 margin at 10x, 0.05% fee per side.
